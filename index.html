Cutting-edge research and development

Igniting the future of propulsion, one millinewton at a time

OUROBOROS is a student-led electric propulsion research team at the University of Alabama in Huntsville. We specialize in the development of low-cost Hall-effect thrusters for small satellites. We are recruiting undergraduate and graduate students from all disciplines to help advance our first five-year design cycle.

We are working to mature a flight-qualifiable Hall thruster design that incorporates advanced features, including magnetic shielding, internal thrust vectoring, and a glow discharge hollow cathode. Optimized for krypton propellant, the final thruster will provide a low-cost alternative to small businesses and university teams.

Why Hall-effect thrusters?

The propulsion technology behind tomorrow's satellites

Hall-effect thrusters offer a unique combination of high efficiency, long operational life, and excellent scalability, making them ideal for modern space missions, especially in the growing small satellite market. Compared to chemical propulsion, they use far less propellant, enabling longer missions and more maneuverability in space. As commercial and academic access to orbit expands, Hall thrusters are emerging as the go-to solution for reliable, scalable, and cost-effective in-space propulsion.
